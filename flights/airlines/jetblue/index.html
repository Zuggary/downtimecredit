What JetBlue commits to, per the dashboard

Yes or no, as the dashboard shows for JetBlue Airways. No means the airline has not committed to it on the dashboard. The commitments apply to delays and cancellations the airline caused, such as maintenance or crew problems.

From JetBlue's own plan

Read by this site on 13 November 2026. Only the passages quoted here were read; the plan may say more.

“If your JetBlue flight is canceled or disrupted due to a controllable irregularity causing an arrival delay of three hours or more, we will provide each ticketed customer with a reasonable meal in the form of a digital voucher.”

JetBlue Airways customer service plan, read 13 November 2026. Open the plan

“If your JetBlue flight is canceled or disrupted due to a controllable irregularity, causing an arrival delay of 3 or more hours, and you are away from your home or origin city overnight, we will provide hotel accommodations at a contracted facility, based on availability.”

JetBlue Airways customer service plan, read 13 November 2026. Open the plan

“If no contracted hotel is available, or if ground transportation is not provided, JetBlue will reimburse reasonable overnight hotel expenses and/or ground transportation with valid receipts.”

JetBlue Airways customer service plan, read 13 November 2026. Open the plan

“Hotels will not be provided or reimbursed for events that are not deemed a controllable irregularity or for customers who live locally.”

JetBlue Airways customer service plan, read 13 November 2026. Open the plan

What applies whatever the airline's plan says

The Department of Transportation says this about refunds, and about asking.

“If an airline cancels a passenger’s flight or makes a significant change in the flight, regardless of the reason, airlines are required to provide a prompt refund to a ticketed passenger, including those with non-refundable tickets, should the passenger choose not to accept the alternative offered, such as rebooking on another flight.”

US Department of Transportation, Airline Cancellation and Delay Dashboard, read 13 November 2026.

“If your flight is experiencing a long delay or is cancelled, ask airline staff if they will pay for meals or a hotel room or compensate you for your time. While some airlines offer these amenities to passengers, others may not provide amenities to stranded passengers.”

US Department of Transportation, Airline Cancellation and Delay Dashboard, read 13 November 2026.
