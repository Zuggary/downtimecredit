What counts as down

      
Microsoft counts a user as down only when they could not send or receive email in Outlook on the web (the browser version of Outlook). Count only those people and that time.

      
Groups of people

      
If some people were down longer than others, add a line for each group. Each group holds different people: count nobody twice.

What counts as down

        
Google's definition: downtime is, for a domain, a period when the user web interface of the service has more than a five percent user error rate, and it is measured based on server side error rate. You cannot measure that yourself. What you type is your own count of how long the service was down; Google's figure may differ.

        
The month

        
The month is the calendar month the downtime started in, counted as its days × 1,440 minutes. The agreement names no time zone. In a month with a clock change the month may be 60 minutes longer or shorter, so near a credit level's edge the statement shows both readings. If the downtime ran past the end of the month, type only the minutes in the month it started in. Minutes in the next month count toward that month, as a separate request.

      

      

        
Bought from a reseller?

        
You can still claim. You notify your reseller, your reseller must notify Google, and you get the credit from your reseller. Choose “Bought from a reseller” in the statement and the request is addressed to them.

        
No fee to type

        
The credit is days of service, so there is no fee and no user count to enter. The agreement gives at most 15 days of service for one service in one calendar month, and 15 days is already its top level.
