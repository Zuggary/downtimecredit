Privacy

Contact: privacy@downtimecredit.com

Short version: what you type stays in your browser. We have no server that receives it, no accounts and no sign-in.

What the site stores

If you save incidents, the list is kept in your own browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you export it to a file yourself. Clearing the list, or your browser's site data, deletes it. If your browser blocks storage, the list lasts only for your visit.

What we receive

Nothing you type. The page does every sum in your browser and loads only its own files. It sets no cookies.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for what it keeps.

Ads

No ads at present, and no ad script loads on the site now. The site has an ads.txt file because ads may be added later. If that happens, this page will say so first.
