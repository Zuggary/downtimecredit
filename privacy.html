Privacy

Who runs this: one independent developer. Not a law firm. No link to any vendor. Contact: privacy@downtimecredit.com.

Short version: what you type stays in your browser. We have no server that receives it, no accounts and no sign-in. A cookie-free counter counts visits.

What the site stores

If you save incidents, the list is kept in your own browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you export it to a file yourself. Clearing the list, or your browser's site data, deletes it. If your browser blocks storage, the list lasts only for your visit.

What we receive

Nothing you type. The page does every sum in your browser. It sets no cookies.

The mail buttons open your own mail program with the text filled in. The page does not send mail and never sees the address you send it to. The record is a file made in your browser and saved on your device.

Counting visits

The home page and the flights pages load one small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is the only file the site loads from another company. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. It does not see your numbers, your request letter or your saved incidents.

The page also counts, as totals, how many visits reach each of these steps: opened the page; started a statement, and whether it was for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or your own contract; saw a result with a credit, or a result with no credit; used the example; opened the request; copied it; downloaded the calendar file; copied a summary, opened a mail to IT or to yourself, or downloaded the record; saved or exported an incident; and whether the visit came from one of our own ads, by the ad's fixed name; on the flights pages: opened the checker or the airline table, saw an answer, picked an airline that the table covers (not which one), copied the request, and whether the visit came from our ground-stop ad. Each step is sent as a fixed word from a short list in the page's code, such as "copied". The word never holds a number, a date, a name or anything else you typed. Cloudflare receives each step as its own request from your browser, the same way it receives the page view. We see only totals for each step: we cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next. It sets no cookie.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for what it keeps.

Ads

No ads at present, and no ad script loads on the site now. The site has an ads.txt file because ads may be added later. If that happens, this page will say so first.
