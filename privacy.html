Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who runs this: one independent developer. Not a law firm. No link to any vendor. Contact: privacy@downtimecredit.com.

Short version: what you type stays in your browser. We have no server that receives it, no accounts and no sign-in. Two outside services may be involved: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and anonymous button totals without cookies, and Google AdSense may show ads and use cookies to do it.

What the site stores

If you save claims on the outage tool (My saved claims), the list is kept in your own browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you export it to a file yourself. Clearing the list, or your browser's site data, deletes it. If your browser blocks storage, the list lasts only for your visit.

What we receive

Nothing you type. The pages do every sum and fill every letter in your browser. A letter you save, print or copy, and a calendar file, are made in your browser and stay on your device. The page itself sets no cookies; ad cookies are covered under Ads.

The mail buttons open your own mail program with the text filled in. The page does not send mail and never sees the address you send it to. The record is a file made in your browser and saved on your device.

Counting visits

The tools, letters and guides load one small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits (this page and the About page do not). Apart from ads (see below), it is the only file the site loads from another company. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. It does not see your numbers, your request letter or your saved incidents.

The page also counts anonymous totals for a fixed list of steps. Each step is one fixed word from a short list in the page's code, such as "copied". The word never holds a number, a date, a name or anything else you typed.

Steps counted on the outage tool

  • Opened the page, and which vendor you started a statement for: Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Atlassian, GitHub or your own contract.
  • Saw a result with a credit, or a result with no credit. The amount is never sent.
  • Used the example, opened the request, copied it, or downloaded the calendar file.
  • Copied a summary, opened a mail to IT or to yourself, pressed Email me this result or Save as a picture, or downloaded the record.
  • Saved or exported an incident.
  • Opened the car-towed guide from the card on the outage tool.
  • Arrived from one of our own ads, by the ad's fixed name.

Steps counted on the home page, the letters and the rule book

  • Which of the four rows on the home page you opened: outage, flight, deposit or paycheck.
  • Started typing into a letter, copied it, saved it as text, or saved its date to your calendar. What you typed and the date are never sent.
  • Downloaded the rule book's CSV file.

Steps counted on the flights pages

  • Opened the checker or the airline table, and saw an answer.
  • Picked an airline that the table covers. The airline is not counted.
  • Copied the request, or arrived from one of our own ads.
  • Used the tabs to move between the outage tool and the flights pages.

Cloudflare receives each step as its own request from your browser, the same way it receives the page view. We see only totals for each step. We cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next. It sets no cookie.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for what it keeps.

Ads

This site is free and may show ads from Google AdSense. Ads pay for the site. They do not change what the tool works out, and no advertiser sees what you type.

Google and its ad partners may use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings, or opt out of personalised ads from other providers at aboutads.info.

The ad cookies come from Google, not from this site. Apart from them, this site sets no cookie. The ads.txt file on this site lists the sellers allowed to sell its ad space.
