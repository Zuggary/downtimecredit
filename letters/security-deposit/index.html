Ask for your security deposit back

Pick your state and type the day you moved out. The letter fills in, with the landlord's deadline in seven states.

Make it yours

What you type stays on this page. Nothing is sent or saved.

Sample dataLetter to your landlord
Landlord's deadline15 March 2026
Thursday 5 March 2026 Oak Property Management, 40 Elm Street, Albany, NY 12207 Re: Security deposit for 5 Elm Street, Apt 2, Albany, NY ($1,200.00) Dear landlord: I vacated the place above and returned possession on Sunday 1 March 2026. I am asking about my security deposit. Please send me, in writing: 1. The amount of the deposit you held. 2. An itemized statement of any amount you kept, with the reason for each item. 3. The remaining balance of the deposit, if any. I understand that New York General Obligations Law section 7-108 provides that, within fourteen days after the tenant has vacated, the landlord is to provide an itemized statement and return any remaining portion of the deposit. I understand the statute also has other conditions and exceptions. Please send the statement and any payment to my new address below. Sincerely, Ana Rivera 12 Birch Road, Troy, NY 12180

This is a sample with made-up details. Type yours and the blanks fill in.

Sample: New York: the landlord's 14 days end, by this site's count: Sunday 15 March 2026.

What to attach

  • A copy of the lease, or the page that shows the deposit amount.
  • Proof of the day you moved out and returned the keys, such as a text or an email.
  • Photos of the place on the day you left.
  • Your new mailing address, in writing (the letter has it).

Where to send it

Send it to the landlord or the property manager in a way that gives you a receipt, such as certified mail or an email you keep. Keep a copy.

The rule behind this letter

The rule, in short: each of the seven states sets its own deadline, from 14 days (New York) to 30 days, and each statute has conditions. Read on 14 November 2026. This site's reading.

StateDeadline in the statuteSource
California21 calendar days after the tenant has vacated, subject to the statute's conditions on the start dateCalifornia Civil Code §1950.5
FloridaIf the landlord does not intend to claim any of the deposit: 15 days to return it. If the landlord intends a claim: 30 days to give written notice by certified mail.Florida Statutes §83.49
Michigan30 days after the termination of occupancy, for the notice of damagesMichigan Compiled Laws §554.609
New York14 days after the tenant has vacatedNew York General Obligations Law §7-108
North Carolina30 days after termination of the tenancy and delivery of possession. The statute allows more time in some cases.North Carolina General Statutes §42-52
Ohio30 days after termination of the rental agreement and delivery of possessionOhio Revised Code §5321.16
TexasOn or before the 30th day after the tenant surrenders the premises, except as another section providesTexas Property Code §§92.103 and 92.104

The sample uses New York. Its rule, word for word:

“Within fourteen days after the tenant has vacated the premises, the landlord shall provide the tenant with an itemized statement indicating the basis for the amount of the deposit retained, if any, and shall return any remaining portion of the deposit to the tenant.”

New York General Obligations Law §7-108, official text, read 14 November 2026.
