  • Claim a service credit after an outageA sample claim for the 12 November Microsoft 365 outage. Pick your vendor and the day it started to see the claim deadline; the calculator fills the letter with your numbers.
    Subject: Service credit claim, Exchange Online, incident EX1234567
Hello Microsoft support,
We ask for a service credit under the Service Level Agreement for Microsoft Online Services (1 November 2026), Exchange Online. Our details, in the order the agreement asks for them:
(i) Description of the Incident: Users could not send or receive email in Outlook on the web. Incident EX1234567.
  • Ask the airline for a refundFor a cancelled or long-delayed flight you chose not to take. Type the flight details; the letter fills in and quotes the rule it rests on.
    Subject: Refund request, booking QX7P2L, flight DL 1234 on Thursday 12 November 2026
Hello,
My flight DL 1234 on Thursday 12 November 2026 from Boston to Atlanta was cancelled. I have chosen not to travel and I do not accept a rebooking, a voucher or travel credit.
Please refund the fare, taxes and fees to my original form of payment, as 14 CFR 260.6 and 260.10 provide for a cancelled flight to, from or within the United States.

How these letters are made

Every sentence that names a rule comes from a statute, a regulation or a vendor agreement this site opened and quoted, with the date it was read. The rule book lists every one. The letters ask; they do not threaten, and they do not say what a court would do. Not legal advice. You send each letter yourself.
