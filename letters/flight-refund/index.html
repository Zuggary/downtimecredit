Ask the airline for a refund

For a cancelled or long-delayed flight you chose not to take. Type the flight details; the letter fills in and quotes the rule it rests on.

What to attach

  • The booking confirmation with the booking reference.
  • The airline's message about the cancellation or delay.
  • Your ticket number, if the receipt shows one.
  • A note of what you were offered (rebooking, voucher) and that you turned it down.

Where to send it

Send it through the airline's refund form or customer service email, or to the address on its contract of carriage. Keep a copy and any case number. Bought from a travel site? Ask the site.

The rule behind this letter

The rule, in short: for a flight to, from or within the US, the airline that took your payment must refund a cancelled or significantly delayed flight you choose not to take; for a flight from the EU, a cancelled flight or a delay of five hours or more gives the choice of reimbursement within seven days. US rule: 14 CFR part 260, text of 1 November 2026. EU: Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 as adopted. Read on 13 November 2026. This site's reading; not legal advice.

A covered carrier that is the merchant of record must provide a full and prompt refund of the airfare, including any taxes and ancillary fees, as set forth in paragraph (a)(2) of this section to a consumer that holds a nonrefundable ticket on a scheduled flight to, from, or within the United States for any cancelled flight or significantly delayed or changed flight where the consumer chooses not to:

14 CFR 260.6(a)(1)

The consumer is scheduled to arrive at the destination airport three or more hours for domestic itineraries or six or more hours for international itineraries after the original scheduled arrival time;

14 CFR 260.2

the refund must be issued promptly in the original form of payment

14 CFR 260.10

reimbursement within seven days, by the means provided for in Article 7(3), of the full cost of the ticket at the price at which it was bought, for the part or parts of the journey not made

Regulation (EC) No 261/2004, Article 8(1)
