Subject: Service credit claim, Exchange Online, incident EX1234567 Hello Microsoft support, We ask for a service credit under the Service Level Agreement for Microsoft Online Services (1 November 2026), Exchange Online. Our details, in the order the agreement asks for them: (i) Description of the Incident: Users could not send or receive email in Outlook on the web. Incident EX1234567. (ii) Time and duration of the downtime: from 2026-11-12 08:00 PT to 2026-11-13 08:00 PT. 50 users for 24 h. (iii) Affected resource names: harborpine.example, all mailboxes. (iv) Number and location of affected users: 50 of our 50 users, in Ohio, USA. (v) Description of errors: Something went wrong. By our count: 72,000 downtime user minutes of 2,160,000 user minutes, an uptime of 96.6666%. Under the agreement's credit table this is a 50% service credit of the $625.00 we paid for the period, which is $312.50. Please confirm you have received this claim. [your name], [your company], [the email address you use for this account]

What to attach

  • The incident ID from your admin center's service health page.
  • When the downtime started and ended, and how many users it hit.
  • The invoice for the month, to show the fee the credit is a share of.
  • Screenshots of the errors users saw.

Where to send it

Send it the way the agreement says: for Microsoft 365 bought direct, a support request in the admin center; bought through a reseller or provider, ask them to file it. A credit lowers a later bill; it is not cash.

The rule behind this letter

The rule, in short: for the 12 November outage, Microsoft must receive the claim by 31 December 2026, by this site's reading of the Service Level Agreement for Microsoft Online Services (dated 1 November 2026, last checked 12 November 2026). Send it by 30 December. A credit lowers a later bill; it is not cash. Not legal advice.

For claims related to all other Services, we must receive the claim by the end of the Applicable Period following the month in which the Incident occurred. For example, if the Incident occurred on February 15th, we must receive the claim and all required information by March 31st.
